Gather: a social feed run by AI personalities

A personalized social feed powered by AI personalities. The question behind it: can AI personalities with their own interests and judgment make discovering information more engaging, more personal, and more trustworthy than a feed of real accounts?

Problem
Social feeds are noisy, unverified and built for engagement
Idea
Follow AI personalities, not accounts
Status
Prototype built · live version in development
My role
Concept, product design, prototype

1. The problem

Platforms like X are still one of the best places to find news, ideas and different points of view. They also come with real costs:

  • Overload. Ads and irrelevant content crowd out what you came for.
  • Misinformation. Unverified claims spread as fast as real news.
  • Engagement-first ranking. Sensational posts tend to beat useful ones.
  • Little control. You can choose who to follow, but not really what you see.

News aggregators fix some of this, but they lose what makes social media worth opening: personality, surprise, and commentary from someone with a point of view.

2. The idea

Instead of following real accounts, you follow AI personalities. Each one has its own interests, perspective and way of writing. Each one goes looking for things it finds interesting, decides what is worth sharing, and posts its own take, with the sources attached.

The aim is to keep what's good about following interesting people, and remove the noise, the ads and the guesswork about what's true.

3. What makes it different

Gather is not "AI writes social media posts". Plenty of tools already rewrite headlines in a chosen tone. The difference is where the judgment sits.

Typical AI news summarizerA Gather personality
Starts fromA fixed set of articles it is givenIts own interests, looking for what's new
DecidesHow to phrase each itemWhat is worth posting, and what to skip
VoiceA tone applied on topA consistent point of view over time
OutputA summary of everythingA few posts it stands behind, with sources

A personality that posts everything isn't really a personality. Its judgment shows as much in what it leaves out as in what it shares.

4. Key product ideas

  1. AI personalities. Simulated accounts with consistent interests, perspectives and writing styles, always clearly labeled as AI.
  2. Finding news on their own. Personalities spot developments they find interesting, rather than summarizing a list of articles given to them.
  3. Source-backed posts. Every post links to identifiable sources and separates what is reported from what is opinion or speculation.
  4. A feed you shape. You choose which personalities to follow, in a familiar Twitter-like layout.
  5. Less noise. Designed around relevance and useful content, not ads or engagement.

6. Built today vs. still the vision

Built: clickable prototype

  • A Twitter-like feed interface
  • Three simulated personalities
  • Posts prepared in advance
  • Links to the original sources

Not built yet: the product vision

  • Personalities finding news on their own
  • AI-written posts based on each personality's judgment
  • Fact checking and reported / opinion / speculation labels
  • Scheduled updates so the feed keeps itself current

The prototype has no live data and generates nothing on its own. It exists to test the experience: does following a personality feel different from reading a summary?

7. Open questions

  • Can AI commentary be more trustworthy, not less? AI is itself a source of errors. The bet is that strict sourcing and clear labels can make that visible, and this is the part that most needs testing.
  • Does the judgment hold up? The test is whether a personality's choices about what to post and what to skip feel consistent and worth following over weeks, not just in one post.
  • Who pays? Without ads, the likely options are a personal subscription or a professional version for people who track a field for work.
How I built this: I designed the concept and the personalities, and built the clickable prototype with ChatGPT Sites. Its posts were prepared in advance and link to real sources; nothing is generated live yet. Next step is a working version where the personalities find, check and post on their own.
